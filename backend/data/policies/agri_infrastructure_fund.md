# Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF)

## Scheme Overview
The Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF) is a Central Sector Scheme providing a medium-to-long term debt financing facility of **₹1 Lakh Crore** for investment in viable projects for post-harvest management infrastructure and community farming assets.

## Eligible Beneficiaries
- Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS)
- Marketing Cooperative Societies
- Farmer Producers Organizations (FPOs)
- Self Help Groups (SHGs) and Joint Liability Groups (JLGs)
- Agricultural Entrepreneurs and Startups
- Central/State agency or Local Body sponsored Public-Private Partnership projects

## Financial Incentives & Benefits
1. **Interest Subvention**:
   - Loans under AIF receive an **interest subvention of 3% per annum** for a maximum period of **7 years**.
   - The interest subvention is available on loan amounts up to **₹2 Crore**.
   - If a project loan exceeds ₹2 Crore, interest subvention is limited to the first ₹2 Crore.
2. **Credit Guarantee Coverage**:
   - Credit guarantee coverage is available under Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE) scheme for loans up to ₹2 Crore without demanding collateral security from borrowers.
   - For FPOs, credit guarantee may also be availed under the scheme created by NABARD.
3. **Repayment Moratorium**:
   - Moratorium on loan repayment ranges from a minimum of 6 months up to a maximum of 2 years.

## Eligible Post-Harvest & Community Assets
- Supply chain services including e-marketing platforms.
- Warehouses, silos, pack-houses, sorting and grading units, cold chains, and ripening chambers.
- Primary processing units (flour mills, pulse dehusking, oil extraction).
- Bio-stimulant production units, organic input production, and farm machinery smart hubs.

## Application Process
- Projects are submitted through the national online portal: **agriinfra.dac.gov.in**.
- Once submitted, preliminary vetting is done by the PMU (Project Management Unit) within 10 days, followed by lending bank appraisal.
