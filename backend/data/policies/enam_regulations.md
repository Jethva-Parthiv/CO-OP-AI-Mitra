# National Agriculture Market (e-NAM) Regulations

## Overview
National Agriculture Market (e-NAM) is a pan-India electronic trading portal that networks the existing APMC (Agricultural Produce Market Committee) mandis to create a unified national market for agricultural commodities. Small Farmers Agribusiness Consortium (SFAC) is the lead agency implementing e-NAM under the aegis of the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare.

## Vision and Objectives
- Promote uniformity in agriculture marketing by streamlining procedures across the integrated markets.
- Remove information asymmetry between buyers and sellers and promote real-time price discovery based on actual demand and supply.
- Provide transparent auction process through online bidding and access to a nationwide market for farmers.
- Ensure timely online payment directly into the bank accounts of farmers.

## Key Reforms Required for Mandis to Integrate with e-NAM
States/UTs seeking integration with e-NAM must enact three specific provisions in their APMC Acts:
1. Single unified trading license valid across the entire State.
2. Single-point levy of market fee across the State.
3. Provision for electronic auction/trading platform across the State mandis.

## Quality Testing and Assay Infrastructure
- To facilitate transparent online bidding where buyers cannot physically inspect goods, e-NAM provides scientific assaying and grading labs at every connected mandi.
- Assayed parameter reports are uploaded alongside the lot number so remote buyers can quote competitive prices with confidence.

## Electronic Warehouse Receipt (e-NWR) Integration
- Warehouses accredited by WDRA (Warehousing Development and Regulatory Authority) are declared as deemed mandis.
- Farmers storing produce in WDRA-registered warehouses can trade their electronic Negotiable Warehouse Receipts (e-NWRs) directly on e-NAM without transporting physical commodities back and forth, saving substantial handling costs.

## Benefits to Farmers and FPOs
- Free registration for farmers on **enam.gov.in** or through mobile app.
- Direct digital payments to bank accounts without middlemen commission cuts.
- FPO trading modules allow farmer producer organizations to upload produce directly from their collection centers without bringing produce to physical APMC yards.
