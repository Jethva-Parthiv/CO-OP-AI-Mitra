# Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)

## Scheme Overview
PM-KISAN is a Central Sector scheme launched by the Government of India in February 2019 to provide financial assistance to landholding farmer families across the country.

## Financial Benefit
- An annual financial benefit of **₹6,000 per year** is provided to eligible farmer families.
- The amount is transferred directly into the bank accounts of the beneficiaries in **three equal four-monthly installments of ₹2,000 each**.
- Installment periods:
  - Period 1: April to July
  - Period 2: August to November
  - Period 3: December to March

## Eligibility Criteria
- All landholding farmer families who have cultivable landholding in their names are eligible.
- A landholder farmer's family is defined as a family consisting of husband, wife, and minor children who own cultivable land as per land records of the respective State/UT.

## Exclusions (Who is NOT Eligible)
1. Institutional landholders.
2. Farmer families holding constitutional posts.
3. Former and present Ministers, MPs, MLAs, MLCs, Mayors of Municipal Corporations, Chairpersons of District Panchayats.
4. All serving or retired officers and employees of Central/State Government Ministries, Departments, and PSUs (excluding Multi-Tasking Staff / Class IV / Group D employees).
5. All superannuated/retired pensioners whose monthly pension is ₹10,000 or more (excluding Group D employees).
6. All persons who paid Income Tax in the last assessment year.
7. Professionals like Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, Chartered Accountants, and Architects registered with professional bodies.

## Mandatory Requirements for Receiving Installments
- **e-KYC**: Mandatory e-KYC via OTP on PM-KISAN portal, biometric authentication at Common Service Centres (CSC), or face authentication on the PM-KISAN mobile app.
- **Aadhaar Seeding**: Aadhaar must be linked to the bank account (Aadhaar Payment Bridge System - APBS).
- **Land Seeding**: Land ownership details must be verified by the State Revenue Department in the portal.

## How to Apply & Check Status
- Self-registration is available online at **pmkisan.gov.in** under the "Farmers Corner" or through CSC / State Agriculture Nodal Officers.
- Farmers can check beneficiary status using their Aadhaar Number or Registration Number on the portal.
- Helpline Number: **155261** / **011-24300606**.
