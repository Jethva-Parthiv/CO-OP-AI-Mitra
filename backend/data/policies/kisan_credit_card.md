# Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Scheme

## Objective
The Kisan Credit Card (KCC) scheme aims to provide adequate and timely credit support from the banking system to farmers for their cultivation and other needs, including crop production expenses, post-harvest expenses, consumption requirements of farmer households, and investment credit for agricultural machinery and allied activities.

## Scope and Coverage
- Covers small and marginal farmers, sharecroppers, tenant farmers, self-help groups (SHGs), and joint liability groups (JLGs).
- Extended to Animal Husbandry, Dairy, and Fisheries farmers for working capital needs up to **₹2,00,000**.

## Loan Limits and Interest Subvention
- General credit limit under KCC is up to **₹3,00,000** for agricultural purposes.
- Base interest rate charged by banks is approximately 9% per annum.
- The Government of India provides an **Interest Subvention of 2%** per annum to banks, reducing the rate to 7% per annum.
- For farmers who make **prompt repayment on or before the due date**, an additional **Prompt Repayment Incentive (PRI) of 3%** is granted.
- Thus, the effective interest rate for prompt paying farmers is only **4% per annum**.
- Collateral-free agricultural loan limit is **₹1,60,000** (raised from ₹1,00,000) without requirement of land mortgage or third-party guarantee.

## Role of Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS)
- Farmers can apply for and receive KCC loans directly through their local **Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS)** affiliated with District Central Cooperative Banks (DCCBs).
- PACS serve as the primary grassroots channel providing interest-subvention loans with zero processing fees up to ₹3 Lakhs.

## Validity and Renewal
- The KCC is valid for **5 years**, subject to annual review.
- Revolving cash credit facility allows multiple withdrawals and repayments within the approved limit.
- Insurance coverage: Eligible KCC holders are covered under personal accident insurance (PAIS) up to ₹50,000 against accidental death or permanent disability.

## Required Documents
1. Filled application form.
2. Two passport size photographs.
3. Identity proof (Aadhaar card, Voter ID, Driving License).
4. Address proof.
5. Land records (Khasra/Khatauni/Pattadar passbook) certified by revenue authorities or lease agreement for tenant farmers.
