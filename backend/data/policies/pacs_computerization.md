# Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS) Computerization & Regulations

## Background and Purpose
Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS) are the village-level cooperative credit institutions forming the grassroots pillar of the short-term cooperative credit structure in India. To enhance transparency, efficiency, financial discipline, and broaden business activities, the Ministry of Cooperation launched the **Centrally Sponsored Project for Computerization of 63,000 functional PACS**.

## Key Objectives of Computerization
1. **Cloud-Based Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP)**: Implementation of a standardized, cloud-based ERP software linking PACS directly with District Central Cooperative Banks (DCCBs) and State Cooperative Banks (StCBs).
2. **Transparency and Auditability**: Automatic record-keeping, real-time transaction tracking, eliminating paper ledger manipulation, and direct seamless audit trails.
3. **Financial Inclusion**: Enabling PACS to offer multi-functional banking and financial services at village doorsteps, including direct benefit transfer (DBT) payouts and loan disbursements.
4. **Language Localization**: The national ERP software supports multiple regional languages so local society managers and farmer members can transact in their native mother tongue.

## Model Bye-Laws & Diversification into Multi-Purpose Services
Under the newly framed Model Bye-Laws by the Ministry of Cooperation, PACS are empowered to undertake more than 25 diversified business activities beyond credit:
- Operating as **Common Service Centres (CSCs)** offering over 300 e-services (Aadhaar update, PAN, bill payments, certificate applications).
- Setting up **Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samriddhi Kendras (PMKSK)** for distribution of fertilizers, seeds, pesticides, and soil health testing.
- Establishing decentralized grain storage godowns under the World's Largest Grain Storage Plan in the Cooperative Sector.
- Operation of retail petrol/diesel pumps, LPG distributorships, and fair price shops (FPS / PDS ration shops).
- Providing Custom Hiring Centres (CHCs) for modern farm machinery and drone spraying services.

## Cooperative Governance and Member Rights
- **One Member, One Vote**: Democratic control where every primary member has equal voting rights irrespective of share capital holding.
- **Audit Mandate**: Mandatory annual statutory audits through approved cooperative audit panels with results tabled before the General Body.
- **Member Grievance Redressal**: Every PACS is required to maintain a member grievance register and provide dispute resolution through the Registrar of Cooperative Societies (RCS) under the State Cooperative Societies Act.
