# Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

## Objective
PMFBY provides comprehensive insurance coverage and financial support to farmers suffering crop loss or damage arising out of unforeseen weather events, pests, and natural diseases, ensuring income stability and encourage continuous farming.

## Premium Rates for Farmers
PMFBY charges a very low and uniform premium from farmers, with the remaining actuarial premium heavily subsidized by the Central and State Governments (50:50 share, 90:10 in North Eastern States):
- **Kharif Crops**: Maximum **2.0%** of the sum insured.
- **Rabi Food & Oilseed Crops**: Maximum **1.5%** of the sum insured.
- **Annual Commercial / Horticultural Crops**: Maximum **5.0%** of the sum insured.
- There is no upper limit on Government subsidy even if the balance premium is 90%.

## Coverage of Risks & Stages of Cultivation
1. **Prevented Sowing / Planting Risk**: Up to 25% of sum insured paid if insured farmer is prevented from sowing due to deficit rainfall or adverse seasonal conditions.
2. **Standing Crop (Sowing to Harvesting)**: Comprehensive risk insurance covers yield losses due to non-preventable risks such as drought, dry spells, flood, inundation, wide-spread pest and disease attack, landslides, fire, cyclone, storm, tempest, and hailstorm.
3. **Post-Harvest Losses**: Coverage available up to a maximum period of **14 days** from harvesting for crops kept in "cut and spread" condition in the field against specific perils of hail, cyclonic rains, and unseasonal rains.
4. **Localized Calamities**: Loss or damage resulting from occurrence of identified localized risks of hailstorm, landslide, inundation, cloudburst, and natural fire.

## Critical Claim Notification Rule (72-Hour Window)
- In case of localized calamities or post-harvest loss, the farmer **must inform the insurance company, bank, PACS, local agriculture department officer, or toll-free number within 72 hours** of the loss event.
- Intimation can also be submitted directly via the **Crop Insurance App (Crop Insurance App by GoI)** or National Crop Insurance Portal (pmfby.gov.in).

## Settlement Timeline & Grievances
- Claims are assessed using Crop Cutting Experiments (CCEs) and satellite imagery / remote sensing.
- Admissible claims are paid directly into the farmer's bank account via electronic transfer within 3 weeks of calculation.
- Toll-free grievance helpline: **14447** or PMFBY portal ticket system.
